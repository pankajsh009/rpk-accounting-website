Our story

Professional expertise, delivered personally.

Welcome to RPK Accounting & Tax Filing Services. Trusted by hundreds of clients across Canada, we provide reliable, proactive financial solutions for individuals, entrepreneurs and growing businesses.

Founded in 2021 by Pankaj Sharma, RPK is built on more than a decade of hands-on public accounting experience. Supported by a dedicated team of experienced professionals, we combine practical guidance, responsive communication and careful year-round support to keep your finances organized and on track.

Whether you need year-end filing or ongoing financial guidance, RPK brings big-firm knowledge together with direct, personal attention—helping you remain compliant, organized and ready for growth.
