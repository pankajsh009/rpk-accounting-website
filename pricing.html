Ask a Question
What can change the starting price?

The number of slips, self-employment or rental schedules, bookkeeping condition, prior-year work and additional reporting requirements can affect the final fee.

Will I know the fee before you start?

Yes. RPK reviews the expected scope and confirms the fee or pricing approach before the engagement begins.

Can I combine bookkeeping, payroll and tax support?

Yes. Small-business packages can be tailored around the services and reporting frequency your business actually needs.

Is the calculator showing my refund?

No. The calculator estimates RPK’s starting preparation fee. It does not estimate your tax refund or balance owing.
